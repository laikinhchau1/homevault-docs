Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

HomeVault is built to keep your information private. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you add — items, photos, receipts, warranties, vehicles, maintenance tasks, reminders, expenses, your name and home address — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us. We have no servers that receive it.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph belongings and receipts, and to scan barcodes, serial numbers and VINs.
  • Photos — to attach pictures you choose from your library.
  • Notifications — to remind you about warranties, maintenance, bills and renewals. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Text recognition (for receipts and serial numbers) runs on your device. You can decline any permission; only the related feature will be unavailable.

Public databases the app contacts

Some features send limited, non-personal information directly from your device to free public services. These requests do not include your name, address, photos or other inventory data.

  • UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com) — the barcode number you scan, to fill in product details.
  • U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (saferproducts.gov) — an item’s brand and an approximate date, to check for product recalls.
  • National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (nhtsa.gov) — a vehicle’s VIN to decode its details, and its make, model and year to check for recalls.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Exports and backups

When you export a PDF report or a backup file, it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it, such as email or cloud storage.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

HomeVault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
