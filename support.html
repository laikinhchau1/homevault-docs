Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. HomeVault has no account and no server, so we can’t see or recover your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Export backup. This creates one file with everything, including photos. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or anywhere you like. On the new phone, install HomeVault and choose Settings → Restore from backup.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app on that device.

If I delete the app, is my data gone?

Yes. Deleting HomeVault removes all of its data from the device. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

How do I create a report for my insurance?

Open Settings → Export inventory report (PDF). The report lists items by room with photos, receipts, serial numbers and values. You can send it to your agent or save a copy in cloud storage.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are turned on in Settings → Notifications inside HomeVault, and for HomeVault in your phone’s system settings. You can choose the time reminders arrive in Settings → Reminder time.

Barcode lookup says the limit was reached.

Product lookup uses a free public database with a daily limit. Try again tomorrow, or enter the details manually.

How do recall checks work?

For items with a brand, HomeVault checks the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) database. For vehicles, it checks the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). Item matches are a best guess, so always confirm with the official notice linked in the app.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language. “System” follows your device language.
